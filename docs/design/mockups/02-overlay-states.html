2 · Capture overlay: states

Everything on the frozen screen after you press Print. The toolbar has the same five modes as macOS ⌘⇧5: screen, window, area, record screen, record area. Shift+Print (full screen) and Alt+Print (window) skip the overlay and capture straight away.

2a · Just pressed Print (area mode)

The screen dims slightly. A magnifier follows the crosshair and shows the pixel grid, coordinates and colour under the cursor. The hint bar fades out after the first few uses. With Remember last selection on, the previous rectangle is already drawn and ⏎ captures it again.
