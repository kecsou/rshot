4 · Screen recording

Pick Record area or Record screen in the toolbar (or press Ctrl+Alt+Shift+R), select the area on the frozen screen, then recording starts on the live screen after a 3-second countdown. Output is MP4 (H.264) with an optional mic track.

4a · Recording in progress

The screen isn't dimmed so you can keep working. A dashed red frame drawn just outside the area shows what's being recorded, and the controls pill sits outside it too, so neither ends up in the video. Stop with the pill, the tray icon, or the same shortcut again. For full-screen recordings the pill can't avoid being filmed, so it collapses into the tray icon after the countdown.
